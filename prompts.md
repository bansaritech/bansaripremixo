## 🧠 Prompting tips for best results

- **Add negatives** to keep photos clean: `no text, no watermark, no hands, no clutter,
  no logos` (unless you want them).
- **Lock a set's look** by reusing the same lighting/background sentence in every product prompt.
- **Aspect ratios:** hero `4:3`, product cards `4:3` (or `1:1`), about `1:1`, packaging `front
  view / vertical`, labels `1:1`.
- **Upscale** the final picks to ~1024px+ for crisp web display, then save into `assets/` with
  the matching filename (e.g. `assets/dhokla.jpg`).
- **Iterate:** generate 3–4 variations, pick the best, then ask the tool to "make a variation"
  of that one.

## 📱 Which app should I use?

**My top recommendation for you → [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) (with its built-in image
generator) or [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com).**
They understand plain-English prompts, handle **text on packaging** better than most, let you
say "make the green darker" in follow-up chat, and are the easiest for a non-technical owner.
Start here.

| App | Best for | Notes | Cost |
|-----|----------|-------|------|
| **ChatGPT (image gen)** ⭐ | Logos, packaging **with text**, quick edits | Conversational edits ("change colour", "add weight box"). Easiest to use. | Free tier + paid (Plus) |
| **Google Gemini** ⭐ | Product photos, packaging, iterations | Great realism, generous free access, understands brand colours well. | Free + paid |
| **Adobe Firefly** | Packaging & logos you'll **print** | Commercially safe, made for designers, good text handling, mockup-friendly. | Free credits + paid |
| **Midjourney** | The most beautiful **food photography** | Stunning results, but text on packaging is weak; runs via web/Discord. | Paid only |
| **Canva (Magic Media)** | All-in-one: generate **and** lay out packaging/label | Generate images *and* add real editable text, logo, FSSAI info in one place. Best for finishing packaging. | Free + Pro |
| **Ideogram** | Logos & **text-heavy** designs | Best-in-class at rendering readable text/typography. | Free + paid |

### Suggested workflow (easiest → polished)
1. **Logo:** generate options in **ChatGPT/Ideogram** → pick one → recreate cleanly as a real
   vector in **Canva** or hand to a designer for an SVG.
2. **Food photos:** generate in **Gemini or Midjourney** → upscale → save into `assets/`.
3. **Packaging:** generate the look in **ChatGPT/Firefly**, then rebuild it in **Canva** so the
   product name, weight, FSSAI number and barcode are **real editable text** (AI-generated text
   is often slightly misspelled — never send AI text straight to a printer).

> ⚠️ **Before printing:** always recreate final packaging text in a proper design tool (Canva,
> Illustrator). Use AI images as the *visual/style*, not the final legal text.
